# Billing dates and invoices

Subscriptions renew on the calendar day the customer first subscribed. If that day does not occur in a month, renewal happens on the month's final day. For example, a subscription started on January 31 renews on February 28 (or February 29 in a leap year).

Customers can download invoices from **Account settings > Billing > Invoice history**. Each invoice is available as a PDF after its payment is processed. A pending payment does not produce an invoice until it succeeds.
