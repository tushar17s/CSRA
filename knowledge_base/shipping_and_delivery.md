# Shipping and delivery

Orders are usually prepared for shipment within two business days. Once a carrier collects a package, the tracking link appears under **Orders > Order details**. Tracking events can take up to 24 hours to appear after the link is created.

If tracking has not changed for five business days, customers should contact support with their order number. Delivery estimates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed. This article does not describe international customs or replacement-order policies.
