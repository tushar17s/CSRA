# Returns and exchanges

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Customers start a return from **Orders > Order details > Start a return**. After the return is approved, the page provides a prepaid shipping label and packing instructions.

Returned items are inspected after they reach the warehouse. Refund processing can take up to ten business days after inspection. Personalized items and gift cards cannot be returned. Exchanges are not available through the return flow; customers should place a new order after starting a return.
