# Cancellations and refunds

Customers can cancel a subscription from **Account settings > Plan > Cancel subscription**. Cancellation stops the next renewal, but paid access remains available until the end of the current billing period.

Subscription payments are generally non-refundable. If a customer was charged twice for the same renewal or sees a charge they do not recognize, support should review the billing record before promising a refund. This article does not define exceptions or refund timelines; ask the billing team to review those cases.
